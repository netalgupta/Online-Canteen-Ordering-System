The Problem We're Solving
✕   Current Pain Points
Long physical queues form at the counter during peak lunch hours
Students wait twice — once to order, then again for preparation
Crowding & congestion around the canteen counter area
Staff struggle to manage high order volume during rush hours
No accurate way for students to know when their food will be ready
✓   Project Objectives
01
Enable digital pre-ordering before students physically reach the canteen
02
Eliminate unnecessary physical waiting time at the counter
03
Provide real-time order status updates and live queue position
04
Improve order coordination and workload management for canteen staff
05
Ensure faster, organised pickup through token-based verification

Step 01–02

Digital Order Placement

Student browses the live menu, selects items, and submits a pre-order digitally from anywhere on campus.

Step 03–04

Queue Calculation

Order is stored in the database; queue position and estimated preparation time are computed and sent to the staff dashboard.

Step 05–06

Live Status Tracking

Staff updates preparation status in real time. Student receives live progress and a ready notification when the order is complete.

Step 07

Token Verification

Each order carries a unique digital token. Staff scans or confirms the token at pickup to prevent incorrect collection.

Step 08

Efficient Pickup

Student walks in only when the order is ready — no standing, no guessing, no crowding around the counter.

Core Principle

Decouple Ordering from Waiting

QueueLess does not reduce kitchen time — it makes waiting invisible by moving it off the physical queue.
