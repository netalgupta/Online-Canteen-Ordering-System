Help & FAQ

Siddhi Services Canteen

How do I place an order?

Choose a menu item, add it to your cart, select a pickup slot and place the order.

How are pickup slots selected?

Slots run every 7 minutes from 6:00 AM. During the day the app shows upcoming valid slots; after closing it starts tomorrow from 6:00 AM.

Can items in one order be ready separately?

Yes. Items can be routed to different counters and tracked independently.

Who marks an item Ready?

Only staff can mark an item Ready. Preparation timers are estimates and never change the status automatically.

Where can I find old orders?

Open Profile → Past Orders or My Orders.

What if something goes wrong?

Use Support or Report a Problem from your Profile.
